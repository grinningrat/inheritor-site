<sup> *Inheritor’s Guide / [[Proficiencies]]* </sup>

Languages, knowledge, and other learned skills are **proficiencies** (🅿). An Inheritor can have a number of proficiencies whose total values are equal to or less than their [[Creating an Inheritor|INT score]].

A proficiency is either **recognized**, **understood**, or **internalized** by an Inheritor: 

- A **recognized** proficiency represents a few days / weeks interaction with it *(ex., knowing locals are speaking Solothish despite not understanding what they’re saying)*. These cost 1 Value.
- An **understood** proficiency represents a few months / years interaction with it *(ex., understanding a conversation in Solothish and being able to speak it passably)*. These cost 3 Value.
- An **internalized** proficiency represents at least half a decade or more interaction with it *(ex., being able to read, write, and speak Solothish nearly as well as a native speaker)*. These cost 5 Value.
## Making Tests with Proficiencies
When a proficiency would apply to a test, apply the following modifiers to the result: 
- [-1] if recognized
- [-2] if understood
- [-3] if internalized
