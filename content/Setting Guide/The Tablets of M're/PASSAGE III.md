<sup> Setting Guide / The Tablets of M’re / [[PASSAGE III]] </sup>

1. Their **Mother** saw each of the Seven in the Way Unfathomable and spoke to them a Word:
    
    1. To thoughtful Va’nir was bound the Word **DREAM**.
        
    2. To quick Anaith was bound the Word **BLADE**.
        
    3. To distant Lyros was bound the Word **VESSEL**.
        
    4. To skillful Zilar was bound the Word **GOLEM**.
        
    5. To wandering Seravil was bound the Word **SPHERE**.
        
    6. To kind Kaliir was bound the Word **WATER**.
        
    7. And to young Nichrae was bound the Word **PATH**.
        
2. All saw their word and knew at once it was theirs. And from then on, even in the Latter Days of the End, whenever these words are used they are suffused with the blessings of Light.
    
3. Then upon the world the Seven raised great houses of stone and metal that captured all the many colors of the world. And the houses of the Solothi were built beneath them, so that they may share in the Light for all time.
