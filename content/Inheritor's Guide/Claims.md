<sup> *Inheritor’s Guide / [[Claims]]* </sup>

The primary path of personal growth is through a Inheritor making **Claims** or forcing others to fail at theirs. A claim asserts authority or influence over something—be it a territory, community, ideal, or any pursuit a Inheritor wishes to command. 
## Making Claims
The subject of the claim must be made aware of an Inheritor’s intention for the claim to be valid. This intent often includes, but isn’t limited to, the **desired result** of the claim, the **length** of time the claim will take to become fulfilled, and any known enemies of the claim. 

Once the claim is made, a Inheritor gains one of the following:

- 1 Hit Die
- +2 to Proficiency value maximum
- +2 to Attunement value maximum
## Fulfilling Claims
Claims are considered fulfilled when they become concrete, widely recognized, or irreversible. Once fulfilled, a claim offers further benefits to the Inheritor who made it. For locations, these benefits are provided in their descriptions. Other common benefits are outlined in Appendix C: Claims.
## Failed Claims
If a claim is earned but then lost, a Inheritor loses any benefits from it but can attempt to reclaim it. If a claim isn’t earned and instead fails, a Inheritor loses their benefits and cannot make another claim until sufficient passing (of time, hands, memory, etc) has occurred.
