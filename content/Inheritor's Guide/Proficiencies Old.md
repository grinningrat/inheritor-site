# Proficiencies List
## Angling
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 2*
The taking of fish by line and net. An Inheritor proficient in Angling knows the waters—where fish gather, when they run, what bait draws them, and what signs indicate a poor catch.

-1 to tests involving fishing, reading water, or judging aquatic creatures.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about the status of bodies of water, catching and fishing operations, and water dependency in coastal and riverine communities.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can find fishing grounds, set lines and nets, read currents and tides, smoke and salt a catch for travel, and know when waters are too dangerous to work.
## Armorer
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 3*
The shaping, fitting, and mending of protective wear. 

An Armorer knows metal and leather, rivets and straps, the difference between a coat that will turn a blade and one that will buckle upon first contact with an enemy. 

An Inheritor proficient as an Armorer can maintain, repair, and assess armor of all kinds. In addition, they know where an armor may have been crafted and how long ago.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about the make and general condition of armor, armored individuals, and workshops or smithies dedicated to armoring.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can repair broken armor through downtime. Without an Armorer, light armor can only be temporarily patched (+1 DR) and heavy armor cannot be repaired at all.
## Bivouacking
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 2*
The art of survival on the road. 

An Inheritor proficient in Bivouacking knows how to make camp in hostile or trackless lands, read the environment for safe passage, find shelter, and keep themselves and others alive when the wilds turn against them.

When a test involves these circumstances, the test difficulty is reduced one step (4d6 becomes 3d6, 3d6 becomes 2d6).

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about terrain in relation to camping, incoming weather, and finding shelter while traveling or lost in the wilderness.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can camp quickly in the wilderness, navigate without roads or landmarks, predict weather changes, and find safe passage through perilous terrain.
## Blacktongue
*Proficiency, Magickal Language, Value 1*
The speech of the Black Iron Faith and the Blackhands who serve the Ebon Lord. 

Blacktongue is a language of command and abasement—each word a nail driven into the world. It is spoken in prayer, in curse, and in the working of Ebon Miracles. To speak it is to acknowledge the Ebon Lord's dominion over all things and eschew any other masters.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about Blacktongue writing, the nature of the text, ebon miracles, the Blackhands, and their great works of Iron.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can speak with Blackhand adherents in their tongue, read and compose Black Iron scripture and liturgy, and invoke Ebon Miracles if attuned to them.
## Brewing
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 2*
The transformation of grain, fruit, and water into something stronger. 

An Inheritor proficient in Brewing knows how to coax ale from barley, wine from grape, and mead from honey. When a test involves these circumstances, the test difficulty is reduced one step (4d6 becomes 3d6, 3d6 becomes 2d6).

Brewing is a prerequisite for [[Proficiencies Old#Stillcraft|Stillcraft]].

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about brewed drinks, how they're made, their purity and general origin, brewing operations, and tavernkeeps and brewers in general.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can brew ale, wine, mead, and other fermented drinks, as well as identify them. Others can attempt crude fermentation, but only a brewer can make something worth drinking.
## Charnelry
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 2*
The labor of the dead. 

An Inheritor proficient in Charnelry knows how to prepare corpses for burial, burning, or preservation, along with what killed them in the first place. It is a grim work, often thankless, but someone must do it.

When a test involves these circumstances, the test difficulty is reduced one step (4d6 becomes 3d6, 3d6 becomes 2d6).

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about cause of death, the state of burial sites and charnel grounds, and the presence of plagues or mass and sweeping death.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can prepare a body for burial or burning, preserve a corpse against rot, render a body down to bone, and perform the common rites that keep the dead at rest.
## Cinderian
*Proficiency, Magickal Language, Value 1*
The tongue of the Cindervale Knights and the remnants of lost Cinderia. 

Cinderian is a language of oaths and fire, spoken in prayer to the Flame Eternal, in Flamekeeper rituals, and among those who still believe Old Cinderia might be reclaimed. 

It is also the language of the magickal Cinder Traditions, incantations of the Ewighflamar.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about Cinderian writing or speech, sites and relics of the Flame Eternal, and about the Cinderian faith and its adherents.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can speak with Cindervale Knights and Flamekeepers in their tongue, read their scripture and records, and incant Cinder Traditions if attuned to them.
## Eldertongue
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 2*
The first language, spoken when the world was young. 

Eldertongue is the speech of the Aelfin before their sundering, predating writing, preserved only in the memory of living things. It is the language of the Mother Moon.

Druids and the Sisters of the Moon keep it still, using it to work lunar incantations and to speak with that which crawls and plants itself into the land without human voice.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about the emotional state of flora and fauna, growing things, blessed places, and the use of Lunar Incantations and the Old Ways.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can speak with beasts and growing things, speak Lunar Incantations if attuned to them, and recognize signs left by those practicing the Old Ways.
## Heraldry
*Proficiency, Knowledge, Value 1*
The reading of blood and banner. 

An Inheritor proficient in Heraldry knows the sigils of noble houses, the colors of knightly orders, the marks of guilds, and the subtle variations that distinguish a true claim from a false one. 

Where lineage and inheritance is power, Heraldry is the language of who owes loyalty to whom.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about sigils and banners, armored individuals and retinues, and the visual manifestation of social and political obligations.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can identify noble houses and knightly orders, trace lineages and successions, and recognize when somone is wearing colors or sigils they have no rights to.
## High Solothish
*Proficiency, Advanced Language, Value 2*

The root tongue from which [[#Solothish]] descends, spoken before the era Sages of the Sapphire Lyceum (pg XX). Compliments the Aelfin Eldertongue (pg XX).

The original language of the Solothi (pg XX), spoken in the [[Timeline#Aelfin Dawn|Aelfin Dawn]] of elden days. Now found only in ancient texts, crumbling inscriptions, and the mouths of Eldlanders who keep the old ways.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about writings in the Eldlands, ruins and relics of that region, and about Eldlanders and the ancient Solothi.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can read texts from before [[Timeline#Thawing|the Thawing]], translate inscriptions that predate modern history, and speak with Eldlanders in their natural tongue. 
## Reckonings
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 1*
The arithmetic of commerce and debt. 

An Inheritor proficient in Reckonings knows ledgers and contracts, interest and exchange, the value of goods across markets, and the subtle ways of striking deals. When a test involves these circumstances, the test difficulty is reduced one step (4d6 becomes 3d6, 3d6 becomes 2d6).

This is separate to the language of [[#Tac|Tac]].

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about goods and trade, contracts and debts, and the transactionary arts of merchants and moneylenders.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can appraise goods accurately, negotiate from a position of knowledge, spot a bad deal, and obfuscate or untangle contractual obligations. 
## Scavenging
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 2*
The art of finding value in what others have abandoned.

An Inheritor proficient in Scavenging knows where to look in ruins, in refuse, and in the pockets of the fallen. Moreover, how to recognize worth in forgotten places.

When a text involves these circumstances, the test difficulty is reduced one step (4d6 becomes 3d6, 3d6 becomes 2d6).

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about items found in ruins and wreckage, discarded or damaged things, identifying found trade goods, and the left-behind belongings of peoples.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can search efficiently during exploration turns, find hidden or overlooked things with ease, judge whether salvage is worth carrying, and stripping corpses and ruins for valuables.
## Scrivening
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 1*
The craft of ink and parchment. 

An Inheritor proficient in Scrivening knows how to copy texts accurately, preserve fragile documents, detect forgeries, and produce their own writing. When a test involves these circumstances, the test difficulty is reduced one step (4d6 becomes 3d6, 3d6 becomes 2d6).

Scrivening is a prerequisite for writing magickal texts.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about the materials and compositions of documents and texts, handwriting and production, and the decay of such things.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can copy texts without errors, produce documents that appear official, detect forgeries and alterations, and preserve writings.
## Snaring
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 2*
The patient art of the trap. 

An Inheritor proficient in Snaring knows how to catch what they cannot chase, how to read animal trails, where to set a snare, how to bait and conceal, and, primarily, how to wait.

When a test involves these circumstances, the test difficulty is reduced one step (4d6 becomes 3d6, 3d6 becomes 2d6).

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about recent signs of moving animals, traps and snares, the appropriate usage of certain traps, and placement of said traps within the terrain.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can set functional snares and traps, predict where roaming animals will go, catch elusive food, and recognize when they're the ones being funneled into a trap.
## Solothish
*Proficiency, Language, Value 1*
The common tongue of antiquated peoples, descended from High Solothish and spoken widely in the Age of Argentum. 

Though the Lords Argent are gone, their language persists—in far-flung communities, among scholars, and in regions where the Old Ways hold. It is a language of the past still living in the present.

Solothish is a prerequisite for High Solothish.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about Solothish speakers and their ways of life, their documents and inscriptions, and how communities of this background function today.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can speak with communities that never adopted Trade, read documents from Lycemic days, and navigate social situations where Trade marks you as an outsider.
## Stillcraft
*Proficiency, Advanced Skill, Value 2*
The liquid mode of alchemy. 

Stillcraft is the art of distillation, extraction, and refinement—transforming base substances into tinctures, poisons, medicines, and stranger things. 

Beyond identifying ingredients, it requires patience, precision, and the prerequisite proficiency [[#Brewing|Brewing]]. 

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about liquid alchemical substances, alchemical equipment and workshops, and the harvesting of ingredients and how to refine them for certain recipes.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can distill alcohol into spirits, extract medicines or poisons from plants and minerals, refine raw materials into alchemical substances, and identify alchemy that has been performed.
## Tac
*Proficiency, Language, Value 1*
A writing system to accompany Trade. 

Tac uses symbols to represent both counting and concepts, written linearly in most official documentation or in grid structures for more complex inscriptions. 

It is the language of ledgers and contracts, ripe for merchants, moneylenders, and reckoners alike.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about the most obvious meaning behind Tac writing, commerical manifests and documents, and how to interpret more complex Tac compositions.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can read and write the numerical and conceptual language of commerce, compose contracts and keep ledgers, and interpret layered meanings in complex inscriptions.

## Tillage
*Proficiency, Knowledge, Value 2*
The working of soil for grain, root, and leaf. 

An Inheritor proficient in Tillage has lived the oldest labor—breaking ground, pulling weeds, praying for rain. Their bodies carry that knowledge with them long after their minds forget. When a test involves these circumstances, the test difficulty is reduced one step (4d6 becomes 3d6, 3d6 becomes 2d6).

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about worked land and ploughed fields, stored food, places afflicted with drought or pestilence, and farming communities in general.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor knows about preparing fields, planting crops, saving Blighted harvests, judging when harvests will be early or late, and the predicted yields of such harvests.
## Trade
*Proficiency, Language, Value 1*
The spoken tongue of the modern world. 

Trade emerged after the Shattering as peoples mingled, merchants wandered, and the old languages fell to time. It is no one's native tongue and everyone's second—simple, practical, and stripped of poetry.

An Inheritor proficient in Trade can speak to just about anyone in populous places.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about overheard conversations, communication, and the general structure of the modern world.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can speak with nearly anyone anywhere, can navigate markets, inns, and roads, and can make themselves understood. Without Trade, one must rely on gesture and luck. 
## Weaponry
*Proficiency, Skill, Value 2*
The wielding and prowess of weaponry

Weapons exist in many shapes and sizes and by their edges have shaped the history of the world. Some mundane and rusted, others gleaming and magickal, all weapons issue a threat to those who witness.

An Inheritor proficient in Weapons can select any weapon and wield it with some level of expertise.

**The Warden provides**
The Warden answers honestly when asked about weapons and where they’re sourced, repairs or maintenance required to keep the weapon in fighting form.

**An Inheritor Attempts**
An Inheritor can speak with anyone on the subject of weaponry, knows when a weapon is damaged or will break soon, and generally what kinds of weapons are efficient against familiar types of enemies
