The second playtest materials packet for Inheritor, called **MOON & MAGICK**, was published on February 24.

It included:

- The [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Magick|Magick]] system
- More [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Attunements|Attunements]]
- More [[Treasure]] in the form of [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Artifacts|Artifacts]] and [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Treasure Tables|Treasure Tables]]
- Fleshing out the Setting Guide, including adding Lore in the form of the **Tablets of M’re**, an in-fiction retelling of the origin of the world.
- Four more locations in [[The Mistlands]].


> [!info] A Note on Future Updates
> Starting with **MOON & MAGICK**, all playtest materials will be published here on <u>inheritorrpg.com</u>. Once all playtests are complete and everything is edited to account for changes, the itch.io page will be updated with final files.
