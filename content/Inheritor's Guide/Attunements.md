# Attunements List
## Blight Sense
*Attunement, Sense, Value 1*
The Inheritor perceives the [[Visitor’s]] influence as a physical sensation such as a metallic taste or a pressure behind the eyes:

- Can detect the presence of Blight, Blight-infused crystals, and Visitor-touched creatures or objects within a small area (a room, corridor, etc).
- Can distinguish between minor Blight contamination and deeper Blight saturation.

This sense cannot be suppressed and is passive.
## Salting
*Attunement, Rite, Value 1*
The Inheritor can invoke the folk practice of laying salt in patterns along a threshold to ward against the unnatural:

- During [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Downtime]] or [[Exploration]], lay a salt ward across a doorway, window, or narrow passage. This takes 10 minutes.
- Undead and cursed creatures that attempt to cross a salt ward must test [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Combat#Morale|Morale]], unable to pass on a failure. Creatures with 3HD or higher roll with [+]. Supernatural creatures are unaffected by salting.
- Persists until physically disturbed (swept away, walked through, rained on, etc.).

**Requires**: [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Trade Goods#Salt|Salt]] (1 pouch).
## Undeath
*Attunement, Mark, Value 2*
The Inheritor has died and returned to the land of the living:

- Undead of 1HD or lower ignore the Inheritor unless the Inheritor attacks them or draws attention to themselves.
- Can see spirits and ghosts that are invisible to others.
- They no longer dream. Sleep is black and immediate.
- Their skin is cold to the touch.
- They no longer restore HP from resting.
