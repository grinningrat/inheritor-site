<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Phases]] / [[Exploration]]* </sup>

Exploration occurs when a Inheritor investigates a location methodically with the intent to make discoveries. This can be done either **in the wilds** (forests, open sea, mountain passes, etc.) or **in the wrought** (ruins, villages, dungeons, etc.). 

In either case, each exploration turn, a Inheritor may move to another area, search the current area, interact with the people or objects in the environment, and more. 

> *Unless the outcome is known, Inheritors should be [[Tests|tested]] while exploring.*
## Exploration Hazards
When an Inheritor succeeds with a consequence or fails their test while exploring, the Warden determines the type of hazard the Inheritor encounters by rolling 1d6 and comparing the result below:
 
| 1d6 | Hazard                                                                        |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | **Encounter**. Someone (or something) is confronted.                          |
| **2**   | **Omen**. A clue to something worth investigating or a danger worth avoiding. |
| **3**   | **Change**. Denizens move. Patrols advance. Weather shifts.                   |
| **4**   | **Consume**. Provisions are depleted. Costs are enacted. Time is lost.        |
| **5**   | **Delay**. Something prevents forward movement.                               |
| **6**   | **Clear**. Exploration goes quietly and unnoticed.                            |

> *If attention is drawn through reckless use of light, sound, or large numbers, the Warden may roll on the Hazard Table with [+].*
