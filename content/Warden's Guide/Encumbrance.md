<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Encumbrance]]* </sup>

An Inheritor has a number of inventory slots equal to their PHY. Items can be **light**, **normal**, or **heavy** for the sake of determining how many inventory slots they occupy.

Clothing, armor, and objects occupy no slots if worn or held.

| Weight        | Inventory Slots     |
| ------------- | ------------------- |
| **Light or Worn** | None                |
| **Normal**        | One Slot            |
| **Heavy**         | Two (or More) Slots |

## Other Circumstances
Modern [[coinage]] of 250 coins or more count as a normal item. 

Items of magickal quality typically weigh one rank lighter than their mundane counterparts.

> The magickal sword **[[Widower]]** is considered light, instead of the normal weight of a mundane, typical sword.
## Overencumbrance
An Inheritor carrying more than their slots allow is **overencumbered** and moves at half-rate. If carrying double or more than allowed, any movement causes **[[exhaustion]]**.
