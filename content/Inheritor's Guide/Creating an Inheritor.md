<sup> *Inheritor’s Guide / [[Creating an Inheritor]]* </sup>

Inheritors have three **attributes**—**Physique**, **Intellect**, and **Ego**—each generated by rolling 3d6:

> [!attrs]
> - **Physique** `PHY` Physical prowess and health. Inventory slots equal PHY. A starting PHY of 15+ grants an additional Hit Die.
> - **Intellect** `INT` Mental tenacity and learnedness. Known proficiencies must have values at or below INT.
> - **Ego** `EGO` Metaphysical keenness and understanding. Attunements must have values at or below EGO.
## Starting HP & Currency
Inheritors begin with a **Hit Die (1d6)** *(HD)* to determine their **Hit Point** *(HP)* Maximum and **3d6 [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Copper Bit (cb)|copper bits]]** *(cb)*. These are rolled prior to play.
## Attribute Loss & Low Results
If Inheritors' attributes drops to 0 or lower, they die. If a player rolls a 5 or lower for any individual attribute or no higher than an 8 across all their attributes, they may discard their Inheritor and create a new one.
