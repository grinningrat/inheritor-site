<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Phases]] / [[Turns]]* </sup>

Time is tracked for the Inheritors in **turns**, though the length of the turn is different according to the phase of the game:

- **Downtime** turns are measured in weeks.
- **Travel** turns are measured in days.
- **Exploration** turns are measured in 10-minute increments.
- **Combat** turns are measured in 10-second increments

If the turn type changes, such as moving from combat to exploration, resolve any incomplete tests before starting a new turn.
