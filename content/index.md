**<span style=font-variant-caps:all-small-caps>INHERITOR</span>** is a dark science-fantasy game where players take on the roles of Inheritors: failed nobles, flagrant merchants, cursed mystics, and errant magicians trying to lay claim to a violent and uncaring world. 

It is written by [Nate Whittington](https://natewhittington.com) and is in active development.
