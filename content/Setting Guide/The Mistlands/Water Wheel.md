<sup> *Setting / [[The Mistlands]] / [[Water Wheel]]* </sup>

The groaning of a dilapidated water wheel rumbles across the valley, an adjoining structure sitting squat besides. The stream that ran through it has become little more than an pitious rivulet, causing the wheel's paddles to start and stop frequently.

Within the structure, half-mad cackles and the sounds of metal tinkering can be heard. Gray-skinned **Silverfisher Argid (Human, 1HD, Hammer as Club, Unarmored, Claim: Water Wheel)** toils here in all manner of artificery. He welcomes any visitors through his workshop of unfinished projects. Gears, silver wire, and clockwork parts lie strewn about in haphazard piles, like a metallic charnel house.

A large, empty frame sits in the center of the room, the only cleared spot of the entire building; Argid claims his "magnum opus"—a **Filigree Knight (Construct, 4HD, 3 x Sword, Plating as Lamellar)**,fully restored from the days of the War of Iron and Silver—up and left in the middle of the night without a sound. He makes it aware that any who bring him clues as to its whereabouts will be well-rewarded for their efforts (200 gl).

Outside, a colony of 2d20 **Silver Eaters (Beast, ½ HD, Acid 1d4, Unarmored)** have made their home in the crevices of the water wheel feasting on the scattered remnants of Argid's tinkering. These miniscule, slug-like creatures devour alchemical metals (silver, gold, others) and expelling a phial's worth of ***metallurgic slime*** each week.

Argid specializes in crafting and trading silver equipment and alchemical metals.

# Claims in Water Wheel
## Water Wheel
>*A slouched building of unknown age, attached to a wheel that churns more air than water these days—though the bones of a workshop are here.*

*Hex, Landmark* 
**Held by**: Silverfisher Argid
**Silver Eater Colony.** The 2d20 **Silver Eaters** nesting in the water wheel produce 1d6 phials of [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Ingredients#Metallurgic Slime|metallurgic slime]] per week, worth 20 sb each. If they are killed or scatter due to being disturbed more than once a week, they do not return and this feature is permanently lost.
**Rivulet**. The stream is nearly dry. Restoring water flow—sourcing from [[Hamdun]]—doubles workshop output and attracts 1d6 denizens per month seeking work. It also floods the silver eater colony, driving them away or killing them outright.
