<sup> *Inheritor’s Guide / [[Tests]]* </sup>

Whenever a Inheritor attempts something where the result is uncertain, the Warden may ask them to make a **Test**. 

When asked to test, the Inheritor rolls a number of six-sided dice, attempting to get a result lower than the Inheritor’s relevant attribute:

> [!attrs] 
> - **Trivial** 1d6 or succeed automatically
> - **Normal** 2d6
> - **Challenging** 3d6
> - **Hard** 4d6 or more

If they roll lower than their score, they succeed. Higher, they fail. If they roll exactly their score, they critically succeed. A roll of 2 or lower is always an automatic success whereas a roll of 18 or higher is always an automatic failure (though not a critical).
## Critical Success
When an Inheritor is tested and rolls exactly their score, they critically succeed on the test. Not only does the Inheritor get a desired outcome from the test, but they may negotiate with the Warden for an additional, appropriate boon.

> *For example, an Inheritor critically succeeds on their test to move unseen among a camp of enemies. The Inheritor asks if they can not only move unseen, but distract the camp away from the leader’s tent—to which the Warden allows.*
### Modifying Tests
Modifiers may affect the outcomes of tests by granting or subtracting dice rolled, granting **advantage** (roll twice and take the better result, shown as **[+]**), or imposing **disadvantage** (roll twice and take the worse result, shown as **[-]**). 

[[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Gear|Gear]], [[Proficiencies Old]], [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Attunements|Attunements]], [[Foundations#Vocation|Vocations]], and more can modify tests, as can the Warden if appropriate. 

> *Effects that grant or subtract dice cancel each other out, as does advantage and disadvantage.*

## When to Call for Tests
As stated above, when an Inheritor attempts something that has uncertain outcomes, a test *can* be called for. 

But how do you know when is appropriate? By working through the **procedure of play**, a Warden can reasonably determine what calls for a test and what does not:

> [!warden] Procedure of Play
> **WHY → WITH WHAT → AT WHAT COST → TO WHAT END**
> 
> - **WHY** are you taking this action?
> - **WITH WHAT** are you using to take this action?
> - **AT WHAT COST** is this action taken?
> - **TO WHAT END** does this action ripple outwards?
