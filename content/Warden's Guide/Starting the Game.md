<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Starting the Game]]* </sup>

Once **Inheritors** have been created, the **Warden** performs the following:
1. **Choose a Starting Location**. This may be done randomly *(by selecting a random hex)* or by asking the players to choose one of the *<u>Starting Locations</u>* listed below.
2. **Determine Followers** *(if any)*. If Inheritors are given [[Companions]], [[Pets]], or [[Mounts]], roll any relevant statistics for them.
3. **Seed Surrounding Hexes**. In each of the six directions away from the starting hex, determine their contents by rolling on tables *(TBD)*.
### Starting Locations
> [!attrs]
> - **Easy Start** The Inheritors begin in the settlement of [[Hamdun]]. 
> 	- The surrounding area is mostly safe and resources are relatively plentiful.
> - **Challenging Start** The Inheritors begin in the covenant-held [[Cindervale Hold]]. 
> 	- The surrounding area is somewhat safe and resources are available for those who know where to look. 
> - **Hard Start** The Inheritors begin at the landmark [[Tree of Song]]. 
> 	- The surrounding area is treacherous and resources are not easy to find. 
> 	- Dangerous [[Covenants]] patrol this area.
