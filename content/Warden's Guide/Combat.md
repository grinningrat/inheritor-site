<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Phases]] / [[Combat]]* </sup>

Combat is comprised of a series of **[[turns]]** *(one for each combatant)* that makes up a **round**. Each round, turns are taken in order from lowest to highest **Defense Rating (DR)** of all combatants. 

> *Companions, mounts, and pets act on the same turn as the combatant they take commands from.*

On an Inheritor’s turn, they can move and make one action, such as making an attack, casting magick, fleeing, or any action deemed reasonable by the Warden.
## Attacking
When attacking, an Inheritor rolls damage dice associated with their weapon or magick. Damage is subtracted from a target's DR, then HP if all DR is depleted while excess damage remains.

When DR reaches 0, it is broken, requiring replacement or repair. Broken armor offers 0 DR.
## Death and Dying
At 0 HP, NPCs and monsters die. An Inheritor dies unless assisted with an action within 2 turns. A single attack reducing max HP to 0 causes immediate death.

If unaware of an attacker, a target can be instantly slain as long as a killing blow can be ensured *(slitting a throat, pushed from a cliff, etc.)*. **Supernatural targets<sup>†</sup>** cannot be killed in this way.
## Recovery
Barring magick or use of healing items, Inheritors, NPCs, and Monsters recover 1 HP per HD daily. If a creature has more than one HD, they gain 1 HP for each HD. 

The creature must be actively resting and pursuing no strenuous activity *(such as combat, traveling, performing magick, etc.)* to restore HP in this way.

No healing can bring back the dead.
## Morale
NPCs and Monsters have **Morale** that determines how likely they are to press on, perform an organized retreat, or flee for their lives when faced with overwhelming odds.

Morale is [[Tests|tested]] in combat by the Warden rolling 2d6 (for the group, not individually) when combatants face overwhelming odds, terrifying circumstances, or begin to lose a battle:

| **2d6** | **Effect**                                                                                   |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2-5     | **Flee for their lives**. Anyone who cannot flee surrenders.                                 |
| 6-8     | **Organized retreat**. Half of the retreating remain as reserves to thwart those in pursuit. |
| 9+      | **Press onward.**                                                                            |
## Wounds
Whenever an Inheritor takes 6 damage to HP from a single source, such as one attack, they gain a **[[Wounds|Wound]]**.
