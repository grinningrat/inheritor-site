## Armor
There are two types of armor: **Light** and **Heavy**. Inheritors, NPCs, and Monsters may instead be **unarmored**.
> [!legend]
> - **Light armor** offers a modest amount of DR. They gain neither [+] nor [−] on tests and allow for a normal range of movement.
> - **Heavy armor** offers a large amount of DR at the expense of [−] on tests that require agility. Some may be constricting, while others are simply cumbersome.
> - **Unarmored** offers no DR, but grants [+] on tests that require speed or deftness. Having normal clothes on or being naked is considered unarmored.

## Weapons
There are three weapon types: **edged**, **pointed**, and **blunt**.

> [!legend]
> - **Edged** weapons are made to inflict cutting injuries against unarmored or lightly-armored foes.
> - **Pointed** weapons are made to inflict penetrating injuries even against heavily armored foes.
> - **Blunt** weapons are made to inflict blunt trauma to internal organs and extremities.

> [!note] Multiple types
> *Some weapons may encompass two or more weapon types and deal damage as any of those types.*
 
**All weapons deal <u>1d6</u> damage**. Weapons may have additional unique features against defenses of a given target.
