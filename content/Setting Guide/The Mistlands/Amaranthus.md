<sup> *Setting / [[The Mistlands]] / [[Amaranthus]]* </sup>

Off the northern coast of the Misted Sea, a purple-sailed frigate skims. Aboard its four decks, 30 **Jeweled Wardens (Undead, 1HD, Sword, Mail)** safeguard precious souljars. **Ruricaan, Jeweled Captain (Undead, 3HD, [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#The Fist of Eru Mal|The Fist of Eru Mal]], [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#The Eyes of Eru Mal|The Eyes of Eru Mal]], Claim: The Amaranthus)** steers them toward the [[Old Silvervein Mine]] at the behest of distant masters whose motives he neither knows nor questions. He and his crew hail from Taklan, the dead city in the far west. Only Ruricaan can speak passable [[Proficiencies Old#Trade|Trade]]; the rest know only [[Proficiencies Old#Taklani|Taklani]].

Beneath the frigate's lower decks, where the souljars lie, a thick fog of **Deathsick** churns. Any living creature that inhales it becomes diseased, unable to heal HP for 3d6 days. If they die while afflicted, they rise 1d6 days later as a **Shambling Dreg (Undead, 1HP, fists as Clubs, Unarmored)**.

All aboard fight until their second death. Should anyone other than Ruricaan break or open a souljar, the spirit’s violent return to the living world has a 1-in-6 chance to unleash **Soulfire**, dealing 2d6 damage to everyone nearby and instantly reducing any undead to ash. Otherwise, the spirit manifests as a **Phantasm Tyrant (Undead, 4HD†, spectral claws as Daggers ignoring DR, incorporeality as Lamellar)**.

The Amaranthus is but one fastship of the Taklan Empire; others sit in distant ports, ready to bury the Mistlands in jeweled dead until the mine is theirs.

Aboard the ship lie treasures of both domestic and exotic origins:

- 400 sp in gathered special coins
- 2 barrels of [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Ingredients#Gravewort|Gravewort]], dried and powdered. Worthless to most, 500 sp to those dealing in charnelry.
- 20 souljars. Worth 5000 sp.
- 2 Taklani rugs, salt-stained but impeccably designed. Worth 1000 sp.
- A *Nightmap*, illuminated only in total darkness, marking two locations along the coast of the Mistlands and one in the Crystal Fangs:
	- [[Old Silvervein Mine]]
	- [[Isle of Gold]]
	- [[The Teeth of Ivir Tal]]
