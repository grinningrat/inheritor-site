An Inheritor moves at different rates depending on the phase of the game they occupy:

| Phase           | Movement Rate Per Turn                              |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Downtime**    | *Ignore*                                            |
| **Travel**      | 18 miles per day by land, 30 miles per day by water |
| **Exploration** | 300 feet per 10 minutes                             |
| **Combat**      | 30 feet per 10 seconds                              |
