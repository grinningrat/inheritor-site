## Designer Notes - Inspirations
*There are, of course, a thousand other inspirations that get the short end of the stick purely due to page restrictions. Suffice to say, if it involves the degradation of the world, ancient and crumbling civilizations, and a morose, introspective tone, it is probably an inspiration if I've read or played it.*
## Designer Notes - What is Inheritor?
*The reasoning behind the World being separate from the Warden is that the setting is not exclusively the responsibility of them. It requires the Characters to engage and enact change upon it as well.*
## Designer Notes - Long Ago, At The Beginning
*SHE was the Mother Moon, the prime deity of the world. She would later abandon the Elfin—and the world as a whole—during the Upheaval. This is why there is no lunar body in the sky above the world.* 
*In the modern age, she is exalted by the Sisters of the Moon and in disparate cults across the Mistlands.*
## Designer Notes - From Then, To Now
*These eras, ranging from the Upheaval to the War of Iron and Silver, differ drastically in their lengths. The Age of Ice, for example, is the longest era at over five thousand years. The youngest is the War of Iron and Silver, lasting only three years.* 
*In time as the game progresses, Characters will find a number of relics and lore on the ages of the past. With each era having a slightly different theme and tone, the Warden is given a palette to choose from depending on the circumstances presented in-game.*
## Designer Notes - Creating a Character
*I settled on Physique, Intellect, and Ego to loosely coordinate to the Body, Mind, and Soul—the connection of our physical, mental, and spiritual aspects. That and they abbreviate well.* 

*Following the ideas here, we can generate a character that we'll use throughout the remainder of the book to help facilitate the rules text:* 

> *Seris is a naturalborn mistlander whose story begins at Cindervale Hold. They have a physique of 9, an intellect of 14, and an ego of 12. This means they have 9 inventory slots, 14 points to put towards proficiencies, and 12 points to put towards attunements.* 
> 
> *They have 1 HD, which they roll at character creation to get a starting maximum of 3 HP.* 
> 
> *They choose an equipment package (Hedge Mage) which offers a mage's staff, a hexer's grimoire, a set of traveling mage's clothes, and a random trinket in the form of a breakbeak skull. They have no armor to speak of, so their defense rating (DR) is 0.* 
> 
> *They then select a number of proficiencies (the languages of Trade (1v), Arcanum (1v), and High Solothish (2v); Stillcraft (3v); Gemcraft (3v); Staves (2v); and Shortblades (2v)) along with a number of attunements (Hexer (3v), along with the hexes Light of the Stable Prism (1v), Fragmentary Spray (1v), Accretive Bloomwall (1v), and Visitor's Angle (2v); Channel (3v); and a Blood Oath (1v)).* 
> 
> *Finally, they choose a vocation: Sensor, which allows them a few tools and a free attunement in the form of Sense. Because of their PHY, they have 9 inventory slots—of which the equipment package takes up 3 slots, with a further slot taken up by their Sensor tools, leaving them 5 empty slots.*
