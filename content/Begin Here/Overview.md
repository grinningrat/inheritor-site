Depending on your role at the table, players and game masters have different responsibilities while playing Inheritor:

**Inheritors are to be...**
- ***Adventurous***. Inheritors have a desire to explore the world.
- ***Changing***. Inheritors change over time, reflecting their exploits.

**Wardens are to be...**
- ***Reactive***. Wardens respond according to the actions taken by Inheritors. 
- ***Immanent***. Wardens approach problems from a 'fiction-first' perspective.

**The World Is To Be...**
- ***Dark***. The World is broken and may never fully recover.
- ***Historic***. The World has a great deal of secrets to be uncovered.
