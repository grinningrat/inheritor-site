A circular shoal extends nearly a mile long and half as wide. Though the water is only a few feet deep, traversing the shallows is made difficult by densely-crowded, rigid saplings topped with glowing, aureate [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Ingredients#Sunleaf|sunleaves]]. 

There is a 1-in-6 chance that 2d10 **Seasingers (Beastman, 2HD, Coral Shard as Dagger, Unarmored)**—mermaid sirens—are here harvesting the leaves in baskets of woven sea foam. As this is a holy site for them, they fall upon any who profane the saplings’ boughs.

At the isle’s center, the water deepens precipitously to a nearly 20-foot drop—the bottom of which holds a ovular tube of silver the size of a carriage, terminated by further, smaller pipes criss-crossing one another. Though the upper part of the tube is glass, it is nearly impossible to see within from the outside. A circular stud opens the glazen windshield.

Within the tube, a seat befitting a human can be found surrounded by levers, further panes of glass, and other strange, inert mechanisms. Behind the seat, a lockbox of pure silver sits unperturbed by the passage of time. The box, held fast by an impenetrable but mundane lock, contains:

- A pristine set of Solothi black-on-black vestments
- 2d10 x 200 [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Platinum drach|platinum drachs]]
- [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Raiment of the Spheres|Raiment of the Spheres]]
- A [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Map|map]] to the [[Gilded Houses]], far to the north in the [[Eldlands]]
