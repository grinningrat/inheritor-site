<sup> Setting Guide / The Tablets of M’re / [[PASSAGE V]] </sup>

1. When Va’nir shared his record with his siblings, there was much tumult within the Gilded Houses. For so committed were the Children of the Moon to their mother that even in the face of Utter Truth, they could not accept nor see it still.
    
    1. *“Blasphemer!”* cried Anaith, fastest to her feet. *“Hold your tongue, and be rid of thoughts of betrayal; lest I cut it from you.”*
        
	    1. Va’nir thought then of his sister’s quick-blade and how she meant her threat truly. For when Anaith spoke of blades, there were no lies between her cuts.
        
    3. Zilar, ever-thinking, felt pity for Va’nir—for he too had questioned some of the silent aspects of the Long Night and its origins.
        
	    1. In similar lines the Aelfin followed: Kaliir and Nichrae of firm belief in their Mother behind Anaith, Zilar and Seravil of compassion and sorrow for their brother Va’nir.
        
2. The world then trembled in a mighty way, as though the roots of it were pulled by unseen hands and fear spread among the Gilded Houses and its many attendants, none so greatly afeared than the Aelfin themselves. 

3. A great tremor shook the gleaming pinnacles of the Elfin halls down to the stone entrances of the Solothi. And so it was that the Aelfin heard their Mother speak in the Way Unfathomable.
    
    1. *“If a question is asked, an answer must be given: I am the only Truth and all who aspire do so through I.* 

    2. *To doubt is to deny the colors, the birdsong, and the air of life around you. Thus, shall they all deny you in return.”*
        
4. The Aelfin quivered in terror but none so much as Va’nir who felt the Light was blinding now, as he could not look upon them without his own glimmering which seeped away as ink in water. 

5. So it was that he departed in fear from them and their houses, retreating to his mindless quarter.
    
6. Yet even in his own sanctum did Va’nir recognize the terror of his affliction, as his fingers began to rot and the sickness of mortality sank its teeth into him.
    
    1. In his illness the wretch spoke to him, still woven into the thoughts and dreams of him: *“This is what your Mother hides from you and your siblings, this unsightly degradation. But this suffering is felt by others as well, beyond the mist.”*
        
    2. And Va’nir replied weakly, but with anger growing in his heart, *“If she hides this, what other darkness does she withhold from us?”*

	3. The wretch then bid Va’nir entreat his far off brother, Lyros, the Great Mariner among them, of which the Solothi knew deep passages beneath the earth to find him. 
	   
	4. So it was that Va’nir descended into the Lower Houses, clad in dark shrouds.
