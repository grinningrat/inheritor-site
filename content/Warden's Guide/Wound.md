An Inheritor may acquire **wounds** during their travels. Unless removed through magickal means, wounds are usually permanent.

| Wound          | Roll | Description                                                                                            |
| -------------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Internal Bleed | 3    | Something inside you is bleeding profusely. Must correct within 2 travel turns or die.                 |
| Lost Leg(s)    | 4    | A leg (or more) was shattered or removed entirely. Movement halved on foot.                            |
| Lost Arm(s)    | 5    | An arm (or more) was shattered or removed entirely. Wielding restricted to however many hands remain.  |
