<sup> Setting Guide / The Tablets of M’re / [[PASSAGE II]] </sup>

1. Of these children, there were seven: **Anaith**, **Kaliir**, **Lyros**, **Nichrae**, **Seravil**, **Va’nir**, and **Zilar**. They were ranked by their coming and in their prowess, as such, it became that Va’nir led them.
    
2. Then the Seven used their Light to restore the secret chambers and their thousand thousands, dredging those who the Light could not reach. They became the **Solothi** and they loved the Seven as elder siblings.
    
3. Then a Light was crafted of beauty and warmth to cast colors upon the darkened lands. Plants and animals were born of the many colors and the motion of the world was restored.
    
4. All saw **Her** as **Mother** and they called her **Moon**.
