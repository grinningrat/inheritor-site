> [!question] Development Pause
> Due to some pressing deadlines, Inheritor development is currently paused and will resume near the end of Summer 2026. Thanks for your support!

The third playtest materials packet for Inheritor, called **TOIL & TENETS**, will be published soon.

It includes:

- Rehauling the [[Proficiencies Old]] system
- Introducing the [[Covenants]] of the Mistlands, factions Inheritors can join and gain or lose reputation with
- [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Ingredients|Ingredients]], [[Livestock & Beasts of Burden]], [[Trade Goods]], and [[Gear]] catalogues
- A [[Wounds]] table for combat and other perilous situations 
- Four more locations in [[The Mistlands]].
