<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Phases]]* </sup>

Inheritors engage with the world in four separate phases:

1. **[[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Downtime]]**, where Inheritors rest, train, carouse, and more.
2. **[[Travel]]**, where Inheritors travel to and explore the unknown.
3. **[[Exploration]]**, where Inheritors investigate room by room.
4. **[[Combat]]**, where Inheritors defend themselves or attack others.

Each phase has its own **turn** length, which dictates what a Inheritor can attempt to accomplish. The Warden determines which phase is active at any given time.
