If you’re starting as a Player *(Inheritor)*…
1. Read the [[Overview]] to get an idea of your role in the game.
2. Begin [[Creating an Inheritor]] and determining your [[Foundations]].
3. Learn how [[Tests]] and [[Claims]] work.
4. *(Optional)* Look at the [[World Map]] and determine any immediate Claims.
5. **Begin play.**

If you’re starting as a Game Master *(Warden)*…
1. Read the [[Overview]] to get an idea of your role in the game.
2. Assist your players in [[Creating an Inheritor]] and assisting with their [[Foundations]].
3. Understand the procedures outlined in the Warden’s Guide, as well as how [[Tests]] and [[Claims]] work.
4. Review the [[World Map]] and the Setting Guide, seeding the map with potential Claims.
5. **Begin play.**
