<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Phases]] / [[Travel]]* </sup>

Travel occurs when an Inheritor [[Movement|moves]] between known locations or ventures into unmapped territory. This travel can be either **routine** or **perilous**:

- **Routine travel** happens automatically. An Inheritor arrives after an appropriate amount of travel [[turns]], consuming [[Equipment#Gear|provisions]] and noting any relevant changes in [[#Weather|weather]] along the way.
- **Perilous travel** requires an Inheritor to navigate [[#Travel Hazards|hazards]]. Travel in this way is [[Tests|tested]] using an Inheritor's relevant attribute.
## Travel Hazards
When an Inheritor fails their test while perilously traveling, the Warden determines the type of hazard the Inheritor encounters by rolling 1d6 and comparing the result below:

| 1d6 | Hazard                                                                    |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | **Lost**. Move in a random direction until location is re-established.    |
| **2-3** | **Threat**. Hostile encounter, natural disaster, supernatural peril, etc. |
| **4-5** | **Complication**. Blocked path, resource depletion, illness, or omen.     |
| **6**   | **Discovery**. Landmark, shelter, useful resource, or sign of passage.    |

## Traveling by Land
While traveling by land, an Inheritor consumes provisions and must spend one day [[Rest|resting]] after traveling for four or more days. Travel beyond four days incurs [[Exhaustion|exhaustion]] for each day traveled.
## Traveling by Water
Aboard a [[Equipment#Water Vehicles|waterborne vehicle]], travel distance is further affected by wind:

| 2d6       | Wind   | Typical Weather                | Effect               |
| --------- | ------ | ------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| **2-3**   | **Calm**   | Clear, Fog                     | None                 |
| **4-10**  | **Breeze** | Clear, Overcast, Storm         | +1d6 miles           |
| **11-12** | **Gale**   | Clear, Overcast, Storm, Severe | +3d6 miles, but Lost |
## Becoming Lost
An Inheritor can see adjacent hexes (and large landmarks up to four hexes away), unless [[#Weather|weather]] blocks the view. Without visible landmarks, travel becomes perilous—test with [−], becoming immediately **Lost (see [[#Travel Hazards|hazard table above]])** on a failure or success with cost (in addition to facing any other Hazards) until you find a landmark or familiar ground.

If an Inheritor is already Lost due to this process, and the Warden rolls Lost on the Hazard Table, they are lost for two travel turns.
## Weather
The Warden establishes the weather for the day. Initial weather is determined randomly. Then, each dawn, they roll 2d6 and compare to the previous day's state.
 
| 2d6   | Initial      | 2-3      | 4-5      | 6-8   | 9-10     | 11-12  | Effect              |
| ----- | ------------ | -------- | -------- | ----- | -------- | ------ | ------------------- |
| **2-3**   | **Clear**    | Clear    | Fog      | Ov.   | Overcast | Storm  | -                   |
| **4-6**   | **Fog**      | Fog      | Overcast | Clear | Overcast | Storm  | Lost unless Routine |
| **7-9**   | **Overcast** | Clear    | Storm    | Storm | Storm    | Severe | -                   |
| **10-11** | **Storm**    | Clear    | Overcast | Storm | Storm    | Severe | Roll Hazards 2x     |
| **12**    | **Severe**   | Overcast | Storm    | Clear | Clear    | Clear  | Roll Hazards 3x     |
