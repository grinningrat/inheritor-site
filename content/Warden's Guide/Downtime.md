An Inheritor performs Downtime in areas of relative safety, such as settlements or [[Claims|claimed]] locations. During this time, an Inheritor can perform any of the following tasks:

- **Improvement**, which represents physical improvement, learning new abilities, or spiritual enrichment.
- **Artifice**, which represents crafting items, developing enchantments, or tasks like painting, developing songs, and performing dances.
- **Fellowship**, which represents carousing in taverns, improving relationships, and establishing institutions.
- **Intrigue**, which represents gathering intelligence, shaping grand designs, or setting plans in motion.

Downtime tasks are [[Tests|tested]] using an Inheritor's relevant attribute.
## Length of Downtime Tasks
Some tasks, like improving a [[Relationships|relationship]], may take one downtime turn. Others, like physical improvement, may take longer. 

The length of time is negotiated between the Inheritor and Warden, based on proficiencies involved, available resources, and potential complications
## Maximum Length of Downtime Tasks
A downtime task can take a maximum of ten downtime turns; any longer than that and the task becomes a [[Claims|claim]].
