<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Exhaustion]]* </sup>

When an Inheritor becomes **exhausted**, they are at the very end of their ability and without rest will surely die. While exhausted:

- [[Movement|Movement]] slows to an agonizing, literal crawl.
- When receiving damage beyond DR, it reduces PHY instead of HP.
- Tests of INT are performed with [-].

Exhaustion remains on an Inheritor until they perform [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Downtime]]. Only after a downtime turn is spent doing nothing other than resting is exhaustion removed.
