Many say magick comes from the stars above, a primal energy radiating outward from strange cosmic engines. Others assert that the ancient [[Mother Moon]] brought magick into the [[Seven]] and, thus, into the world by their grace. The truth is that magick has always existed here; since before the days of the [[Timeline#Aelfin Dawn|Aelfin Dawn]], before the stars flared between life and death, when Spells—and their thralls—ruled all.

Now, in this fallen age, magick has withdrawn from the world entire — yet its vestiges remain. The only magick one may hope to encounter are the lesser magicks stored within **[[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Artifacts|Artifacts]]**, themselves often storied beyond measure, and the impossible, hidden **Spells**.

There are but fifty Spells in our world. Each is a mind of its own, with its own will and desires. All wish to hasten the coming end of all ages and erase the knowledge of magick in the world. Each despises all others of its kind. Each are named, though whether they chose these names or were given them none can say.
# Spells
## Bearing a Spell
Like all [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Attunements|attunements]], Spells have an **attunement value**. An Inheritor must have sufficient attunement budget to bear it. Most Spells demand so much of their bearer that carrying one leaves room for little else.
## Using a Spell
When an Inheritor calls upon their Spell's power, they [[Tests|test]] EGO: 

- **On a success**, the power manifests as the bearer intends. 
- **On a failure**, the power still manifests—but the Spell’s will overpowers the Inheritor’s. The Warden describes how the Spell twists the bearer's intent toward its own desire.
## Loss of Control
The Warden may call for an EGO [[Tests|test]] whenever the Spell sees an opportunity to exert its will upon its bearer. At minimum, the Spell pushes once per [[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Downtime]] phase. The difficulty of the test is determined by the bearer's circumstances. 

- **On a success**, the Inheritor resists and carries on. 
- **On a failure (and all subsequent failures)**, the Spell seizes control:

| Failures                      | **First Failure**                  | **Second Failure**                      | **Third Failure**               | **Fourth Failure**                 | **Fifth Failure**                       |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Duration of Spell’s Dominance | A single combat turn (~10 seconds) | A single exploration turn (~10 minutes) | A single travel turn (1d6 days) | A single downtime turn (1d6 weeks) | **Permanent**. *The Inheritor is lost.* |

During this time, the Spell acts through the bearer in pursuit of its desires. The bearer is aware, but unable to intervene until the end of the duration.
# Spell List
- **Attunement Value**: See [[Creating an Inheritor]].
- **True Form**: How the Spell manifests to the bearer and any viewers outside of its preferred form.
- **Preferred Form**: How the spell wishes to manifest to avoid detection.
- **Desire**: The Spell’s whims and personal goals (all Spells work towards the world’s end, the death of all other Spells, and the loss of widespread magickal knowledge)
- **Power**: The Spell’s abilities that can be invoked by the bearer.
- **Corruption**: How the Spell’s domination over the bearer may manifest.
