<sup> *Warden’s Guide / [[Relationships]]* </sup>

A **relationship** is the bond between an NPC and an Inheritor; bound by debt, gratitude, suspicion, or blood. When an Inheritor gains a relationship, they (along with the Warden) describe who they are, how they know them, and whether they are **Friendly**, **Suspicious**, or **Hostile**. 
## Requesting Favors
An Inheritor can request a Favor from their relationship:
- **Friendly**. The Inheritor tests the relationship and, if successful, the favor is made. The relationship becomes Suspicious if the test is failed or the result is less than the total number of favors made by the relationship.
- **Suspicious**: The Inheritor tests the relationship with [−] and, if successful, the favor is made. The relationship becomes Hostile if the test is failed or the result is less than the total number of favors made by the relationship.
- **Hostile**: Hostile relationships make no favors. They actively act against an Inheritor wherever possible.
## Other Relationship Changes
Relationships can change in other ways. Some examples:

| Action                 | Potential Shift     |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Take for Granted**       | Friendly → Suspicious   |
| **Betray Trust**           | Friendly → Hostile      |
| **Make Amenable Amends**   | Hostile → Suspicious    |
| **Prove Yourself to Them** | Suspicious → Suspicious |
| **Ignore What You Owe**    | Suspicious → Hostile    |
| **Save Their Life**        | Hostile → Suspicious    |
