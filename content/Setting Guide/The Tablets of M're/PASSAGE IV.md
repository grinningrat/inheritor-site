<sup> Setting Guide / The Tablets of M’re / [[PASSAGE IV]] </sup>

1. Then the Seven were visited by a wretch of the Lands Beyond the Sea in the misted places where colors could not reach. He came to them, naked and soot-stained, in their gilded houses and bore the Word **IRON** upon his soul.
    
    1. *“In Two Words I can unmake thee”*, spoke the shade and Anaith split him in one hundred and seven ways with a single blade-stroke.
        
    2. *“In Two Words I can shatter your works”*, spoke the essence of the shade, which had buried itself in the colors of the sunset cast long upon the houses and Seravil banished him from their lands with a spell whose words cannot be spoken.
        
    3. *“In Two Words I can set you free”*, heard the Seven in their shared-mind where they could not excise and Kalios mused:
        
        1. *“A memetic thought made corporeal, bearing a True Word, now is part of the fabric of our shared-mind. Shall we kill one another and begin again?”*
            
        2. Next spoke Nichrae, excitedly, *“There may be more to this wretch and his Lands Beyond the Sea. We should traverse the mist and set upon them in the thousands carving color all the while!”*
            
        3. Lyros left citing words of deep discomfort at the intrusion and shattered the crystal in his heart, ending his shared-mind connection.
	        
2. Va’nir was deeply troubled by the shade and later returned to his mindless quarter, where none of his siblings could reach for it was in Dream.
    
	1. To his **Mother** he stated, *“There are no coincidences, only insight.”*
    
	2. His **Mother** smiled in the Way Unfathomable and said *“Insight is but one path.”*
    
	3. In his mind’s mind Va’nir heard the wretch whisper _“Are there not paths unending? What Mother would mimic cruelty as insight?”_
    
3. Then Va’nir felt fear and he fashioned a record with his mind in the ninth position so as not to further invite the influence of others in his writing. When he finished he had written two thousand words within which the spaces between spelled Utter Truth.
    
	1. Va’nir looked upon it and wept, for it read the First Beginning and the Final End and both were by his **Mother’s** hand.
