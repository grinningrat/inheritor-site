<sup> *Setting Guide / [[The Mistlands]]* </sup>

Most speak [[Proficiencies Old#Trade|Trade]]. Near the [[Crystal Fangs]] and [[Echolands]], [[Solothish]] is common. [[Black Iron Faithful]] speak and write [[Blacktongue]]. [[Sisters of the Moon]] speak [[Eldertongue]] and write in [[Arcanum]], as all magic-wielders do.
## Random Encounters
Roll on the following table every 12 hours regardless of location—determine the goals of arriving parties based on location.

> If no statistics are provided for a creature, assume they are a **Commoner (Human, 1HD, Unarmed, Unarmored)**.

| **1d100** | **Encounter**                                                                                                                            |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1-5**   | 2d20 **Ironmad (Undead, 2HD, Fists as Clubs, Skin as Jack)**                                                                             |
| **6-10**  | 3 **Sisters of the Moon (Human, 2HD, Grimoire *3, Lunar* War Sickle, Unarmored)** and 1d4 **Postulants (Human, 1HD, Dagger, Unarmored)** |
| **11-13** | 1d4 **Crystal Pilgrims (Human, 1HD, Grimoire *1, Crystal*, Dagger, Unarmored)** hiding their Blight in voluminous robes                  |
| **14-16** | 1 Merchant and 1d4 **Hired Swords (Human, 2HD, Sword, Jack)**                                                                            |
| **17-18** | 2d20 **Wild Horses (Beast, 1HD, Hooves as Clubs, Unarmored)**; 1-in-6 chance at Mistborn Stallion leading them                           |
| **19-20** | 1d100 Livestock, 1 Herdsman per 20                                                                                                       |
| **21-24** | Conflict; roll 1d20 twice to determine each side.                                                                                        |
| **25-30** | Corpse; roll 1d20 to determine origin.                                                                                                   |
| **31-50** | 1 **Blacktongue Priestess (Human, 2HD, Prayer Book *2, Iron* Mace, Unarmored)** led by 2d4 Ironmad like chained dogs                     |
