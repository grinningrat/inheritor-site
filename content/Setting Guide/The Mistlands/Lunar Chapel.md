An ancient stone monument hides shrouded within the dense wood, older than the trees that surround it; a crumbling relic, its pillars and steps lined with fading witch-hexes. At its heart stands an ash-white statue of a woman—head missing, right hand raised, left hand low; both palms stained black.

Shadows haunt here; a group of seven **Dark Sisters (Human, 2HD, *[[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Magick Items#Runeward Dagger|Runeward Dagger]]*, Unarmored, Claim: Lunar Chapel)** of varied ages and wiry frames that belie their unnatural strength. The Sisters revere the **Mother Moon** as a divine, lupine woman, whom they believe departed the heavens to hunt her cosmic enemies.

Once devout **Sisters of the Moon**, they turned to sacrilege, supping the blood of one another and that of captured victims in fell rituals. These rites have corrupted them, giving them a feral hunger for flesh and a dark mastery of hexes. They await the moon's return to the skies above—from her maw a torrent of blood is to flood the world, drowning those found unworthy. 

Any approaching woman may have a short window to speak with the Sisters before they turn on her. Any approaching man is attacked long before he sees them.

A short distance from the chapel lies a shallow mass grave, within which lies hidden cache of mismatched items, stolen or salvaged during the sisters' hunts:

- Faded love letters, still faintly smelling of [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Ingredients#Elia Petal|elia petals]]
- 2 x phials of [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Ingredients#Iron-Shaled Blood|iron-shaled blood]]
- A dusty vial of [[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Gear#Asterial Concoction|asterial concoction]], stamped with a crescent moon
# Lunar Chapel Encounters
In addition to the [[The Mistlands#Random Encounters|Mistlands Random Encounters]], you may supplement them with the following table of Lunar Chapel-specific situations. 

*Roll 2d6:*

|    Encounter     | Description                                                                                                                   |
| :--------------: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  **2: Ritual**   | All the Sisters gather at the statue, bleeding into a stone basin.                                                            |
|  **3-4: Hunt**   | 1d4 Sisters drag a bound captive to the chapel. 1-in-6 chance they’re still alive, muffled and kicking.                       |
| **5-6: Patrol**  | 1d4 Sisters patrol the outskirts. 1-in-6 chance they miss oncomers.                                                           |
| **7-8: Feeding** | 1 starved Sister distractedly drinks from a corpse near the tree line.                                                        |
|  **9-10: Omen**  | 1d4 Sisters kneel, silent, staring at the sky. Between them, a young, flayed **Postulant** sing-shrieking in fits and starts. |
| **11: Discord**  | The Sisters are divided over a captive’s fate. They are all distracted.                                                       |
| **12: Silence**  | The chapel is empty. The Sisters are elsewhere; returning on a 1-in-6 each [[Turns\|Turn]].                                   |
# Claims in Lunar Chapel
## Lunar Chapel

>*A forgotten temple to the Mother Moon. Foul transgressions have been enacted upon the stone; all before the statue of a woman’s form in holy posture.*

*Hex, Landmark* 
**Held by**: Dark Sisters
**Mother Statue.** Once per downtime turn, an offering of blood at the statue’s basin grants a single casting of a Lunar Magick incantation (roll randomly).
**Witch-Hex Wards.** As long as one phial of blood is offered to the statue per downtime turn, any enemy within 2 hexes must test EGO or be revealed to the Claim holder.
