Hollow chimes echo before the flooded village is seen. A temple—half-rendered incorporeal—shadows an abandoned courtyard. Squat hovels radiate outwards from the temple, all built for smaller frames, doorways encrusted with mucus and salt. The windowless temple's walls are carved with spiraling grooves meant for webbed fingers to trace in prayer. The entire village is submerged in water up to the knees, with sheer dropoffs into inky blackness at the outskirts.

80 spirits orbit the temple exterior, gathering around visitors, gesturing to a descending passage within the holy place. Unable to speak, they cannot explain why they encircle without entering—only that something terrible resides below. 

Past the hovels, hidden from the courtyard, rows of crucified creatures of all kin hang submerged to their necks, their bodies stripped to bone.

They owe this degradation to **Y'rtul the Bloodletter (Beastman, 4HD, *[[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Bloodletter’s Pike|Bloodletter’s Pike]]*, Cuirass)**, a vicious merman who hunts the local waterways. There is a 20% chance he is already here, otherwise he returns on a 2-in-6 per turn.

Y'rtul serves **Aracioth (Undead Dragon, 7HD†[−], Curse Breath 3d6, Claws as War Sickles, Scales as Lamellar, Claim: Greenbury)**, a wingless drake laired in the temple's flooded underbelly, his scales warped and corroded by black iron.

Aracioth rarely leaves, relying on Y'rtul to bring souls to sustain him. Y'rtul believes these sacrifices will grant him dominion over the waterways by way of favor with the dragon. Aracioth despises needing the merman and plans to kill him once strong enough.

Aracioth's hoard contains:
- Loose copper bits and silver bobs, totaling 300 cb in value
- ***[[Inheritor/Warden's Guide/Treasure#Spectral Chainmail|Spectral Chainmail]]***
- A **Weeping Ironsore (Plant, 5HD[+], Cannot Attack, Skin as Mail)** expelling 1d6 phials of ***[[Inheritor/Inheritor's Guide/Ingredients#Iron Grease|iron grease]]*** each day until killed
