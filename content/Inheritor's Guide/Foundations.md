An Inheritor’s **Foundations** are the circumstances that shaped them before play begins.

Determine them in order:

1. **Origin**: The nature of the Inheritor’s birth.
2. **Vocation**: The training that made them.
3. **Events**: The obstacles that marked them.
## Aging
Inheritors begin at **Age** 0. As they gain Vocations and survive Events, they gain years.

**After age 40**, whenever years are added, make a [[Tests|challenging test]] against both PHY and INT:

- *Above attribute*: the Attribute decreases by 1.
- *Equal to or below*: no change.

**After age 50**, these [[tests]] are made with disadvantage[-].
**After age 60**, both Attributes automatically decrease by 1 each time years are added.
## Origin
An Inheritor's **Origin** determines which **Starsign** they were born under. Each Starsign grants a permanent effect.

All Origins add +10 years to Age.

## Events
Moments that changed a Inheritor’s trajectory. 

Each Inheritor rolls on the tables specified by their Origin or Vocation, and then on the Life Events table for every 10 years they've been alive.
